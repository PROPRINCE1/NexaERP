A clearer view of everyday business.

Practical operations intelligence for growing Pakistani businesses.

NexaERP AI is a proposed student venture exploring how routine stock, sales and purchasing records could work together to help small teams make informed decisions.

Explore proposed services View the project concept

The idea

Business records should tell one story.

In some growing businesses, sales, purchases and stock live in separate spreadsheets, paper books or apps. NexaERP AI is exploring a more connected way to keep those records understandable and useful.

The proposal starts with routine operations: keeping product records, noting stock movements, recording transactions and reviewing summaries.

Who it is for

Small Pakistani retail, wholesale and distribution businesses with repeated stock movements and disconnected operational records.

Who might use it

Owners and finance or operations managers could review reports; inventory, sales, accounts and store staff could maintain daily records.

NexaERP AI is a working name for a student venture concept, not a registered company or commercially available service.

Proposed project targets

What the team aims to validate and build

Not achieved results

  • Validate at least three user workflows through interviews before requirements freeze.

  • Deliver a prototype covering four core workflows by the end of month 6.

  • Achieve at least 80% task completion in a small usability study with at least five representative participants.

  • Evaluate forecasts against a seasonal-naive baseline using a time-based holdout.

  • Complete a security and data-quality checklist before pilot testing.
